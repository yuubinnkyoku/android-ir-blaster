# DP-700SH investigation log — 2026-09-23 14:02 JST

## Search performed

Continued searches for DP-700SH/850SH/1020SH firmware binaries, board/SoC evidence, remote IR data, and Sharp/ODM lineage. Generic web search produced no new direct evidence for the target binaries or IR codes.

The useful lead came from walking the archived FUJIFILM DP-70SH firmware pages in Wayback rather than relying on indexed search results.

## Archive path recovered

1. Queried the old FUJIFILM DP-70SH support subtree.
2. Located archived `download002.html`, the page shown after accepting the firmware license.
3. Recovered the official predecessor firmware name and URLs: `TH180700.ver` (1.9MB), under `/pub/tools/dp70sh/`.
4. Queried the exact binary URL in Wayback CDX.
5. Found two archived `application/octet-stream` captures from 2016-06-23/24.
6. Retrieved the archived firmware and inspected visible strings exposed by the raw snapshot.

## Key observations

Direct strings include `SHARP_PF100_PLATFORM`, `NEW SERIAL FLASH`, `FW Revision: V%d.%02d`, `BOOT`, `NVRAM`, `ROMTAB`, `STRTAB`, `FIRMWARE`, `VERSION`, `CHECKSUM`, `ATMEL`, `MXIC`, numerous USB stack/debug messages, and build timestamp `13:21:49 Apr 23 2009`.

This is the first recovered executable firmware image in the immediate DP-70SH -> DP-700SH family and provides a concrete binary reference for later comparison against `TH34_dpf.pkg` or an on-device DP-700SH flash dump.
