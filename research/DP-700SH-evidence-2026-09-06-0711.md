## New evidence: SanJet trademark filing explicitly covered digital photo frames immediately after AIPTEK manufacturing-business transfer

Taiwan trademark data sourced from the Ministry of Economic Affairs Intellectual Property Office and mirrored by FindCompany records a SanJet/勝捷 trademark filing with the following details:

- Applicant / owner: 勝捷光電股份有限公司 (SanJet Technology Corp.)
- Trademark: 勝捷
- Application no.: `098027401`
- Registration no.: `01391873`
- Application date: **2009-06-26**
- Registration date: **2010-01-01**
- Nice class: 009
- Listed goods include **數位相框 (digital photo frames)**, along with digital cameras, digital photo players, portable media players, MP3 players, digital camcorders, etc.

Source:
- https://www.findcompany.com.tw/trademark/01391873_098027401

This filing occurred only 25 days after the 2009-06-01 effective date of AIPTEK/天瀚科技's transfer of its ODM/manufacturing business to the already-existing SanJet corporation, and 23 days after SanJet's 2009-06-03 importer/exporter registration.

Company-registration cross-check:
- https://www.findcompany.com.tw/en/SANJET%20TECHNOLOGY%20CORP.

### Interpretation / circumstantial evidence

The trademark filing is stronger than a later company-profile claim that SanJet handled digital photo frames: it shows that **digital photo frames were explicitly within SanJet's intended Class-009 product scope almost immediately after the AIPTEK manufacturing-business transfer**.

This improves the chronology behind the working hypothesis:

`AIPTEK DPF / multimedia development assets -> 2009-06 business transfer to SanJet -> SanJet explicitly claims digital photo frames in trademark goods on 2009-06-26 -> 2009-10 to 2010-02 Sharp-customer FUJIFILM DP-850SH/DP-1020SH engineering record`

The timing makes it more plausible that DPF capability was part of the business/assets SanJet was preparing to commercialize after the transfer rather than a product category added much later.

### Not proven / do not overstate

- The trademark does **not** prove SanJet designed or manufactured DP-700SH / DP-850SH / DP-1020SH.
- It does **not** identify the SoC, OS, PCB, firmware format, IR protocol, factory, or Sharp/SanJet contract.
- It does **not** prove that the digital-photo-frame goods listed in the trademark came specifically from the AIPTEK-transferred business, only that SanJet formally claimed that product category on 2009-06-26.
