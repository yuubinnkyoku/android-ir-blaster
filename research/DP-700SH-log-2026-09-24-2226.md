## Meaningful new result

Sharp's official HN-PP150 update procedure explicitly states that `TH150400.ver` cannot be applied to HN-PP100. Since eSOL independently confirms both HN-PP100 and HN-PP150 used eCROS / PrKERNELv4, this provides a concrete precedent for model-specific, non-interchangeable firmware inside a shared Sharp software family.

This strengthens a cautious interpretation of FUJIFILM's three separate 2010 packages (`TH34_dpf.pkg`, `TH35_dpf.pkg`, `TH36_dpf.pkg`): separate packages do not argue against a shared OS/base, but also cannot be used to claim identical hardware.
