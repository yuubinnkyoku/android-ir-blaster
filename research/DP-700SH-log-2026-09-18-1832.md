No new raw/LIRC/Pronto/Flipper capture for `RRMCG2009SCZZ` was found. Existing evidence that all buttons emit IR and that the 2010 models likely share the remote remains unchanged.

### Board / SoC

No DP-700SH/850SH/1020SH teardown with legible main-board IC markings was found.

A new historically relevant reference-design lead was found instead:

- CTIMES, 2007-05-29: XGI (繪展科技) + Genesys Logic DPF solution using XGI `3210` + Genesys `GL824`.
- XGI `3210` supports 2D/JPEG/Motion JPEG/MPEG4/MP3 hardware acceleration.
- GL824 supplies USB 2.0 OTG and flash-card functions.
- Multiple customers had adopted the solution.
- ITE later absorbed XGI and publicly continued DPF SoC work using XGI graphics technology; Huang Bo-wei appears in both the 2007 XGI announcement and the 2010 ITE DPF article.

This is logged in `research/DP-700SH-evidence-2026-09-18-1832.md`.

### IT9834 / IT9836 caution

Fresh search found modern ITE official IT983x documentation (2021 era), but no dated 2009/2010 primary source assigning `IT9834` or `IT9836` to the DPF SoC discussed at that time. Modern IT9834 is documented for display up to 480×480, while DP-700SH/850SH are 800×480. Treat the historical `IT9834/IT9836` identification as unconfirmed and potentially an anachronistic model-number conflation until direct evidence appears.
