# DP-700SH investigation evidence — 2026-09-24 22:26 JST

## New evidence: Sharp HN-PP150 update explicitly excludes HN-PP100

### Confirmed

Sharp's surviving official firmware update procedure for the AQUOS Photo Player HN-PP150 explicitly says that the update firmware `TH150400.ver` is for **HN-PP150** and **cannot be applied to HN-PP100**.

The same document instructs users to copy `TH150400.ver` to the root directory of an SD/SDHC card. Sharp's firmware download page identifies the file as `TH150400.ver` and gives its size as 121 MB; the target firmware version is 1.03.01.04 or earlier.

Sources:
- Sharp firmware download page: https://jp.sharp/support/photoplayer/fw_agree.html
- Sharp update procedure PDF: https://jp.sharp/support/photoplayer/doc/how_to_update.pdf

Independent earlier evidence establishes that both HN-PP100 and HN-PP150 used eSOL eCROS centered on the μITRON 4.0-compatible PrKERNELv4, with eBinder, file systems, media drivers and a USB host stack.

Source:
- eSOL / ValuePress announcement (2009-05-12): https://www.value-press.com/pressrelease/38910

### Interpretation / hypothesis

This is useful as a constraint on the DP-series comparison. Even two Sharp photo-player products that are confirmed to share the same eCROS/PrKERNELv4 software family did **not** necessarily share a directly interchangeable firmware image. Therefore the DP-700SH/850SH/1020SH split into `TH34_dpf.pkg`, `TH35_dpf.pkg`, and `TH36_dpf.pkg` is compatible with a shared OS/software base while still allowing model-specific hardware configuration, drivers, resource tables, or application images.

The `TH*.ver` naming seen in DP-70SH (`TH180700.ver`) and HN-PP150 (`TH150400.ver`) remains a useful Sharp-family clue, but the filename prefix/suffix alone must not be treated as proof of an identical container format, SoC, board, or OS.

### Not established

- No evidence yet that DP-70SH / DP-700SH uses eCROS or PrKERNELv4.
- No evidence that `TH180700.ver` and `TH150400.ver` have the same binary container format.
- No evidence that HN-PP100/HN-PP150 and DP-700SH share a SoC or PCB.
- `TH34_dpf.pkg`, `TH35_dpf.pkg`, and `TH36_dpf.pkg` binaries remain unrecovered in this run.
- No new raw IR codes or SoC-marking PCB photos were found in this run.
