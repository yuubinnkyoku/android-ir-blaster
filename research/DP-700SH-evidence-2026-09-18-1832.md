# DP-700SH evidence — 2026-09-18 18:32 JST

## New evidence: historically plausible pre-ITE DPF reference design from XGI + Genesys Logic

### Confirmed facts

A 2007-05-29 CTIMES report documents a digital-photo-frame reference solution jointly announced by **XGI Technology (繪展科技)** and **Genesys Logic (創惟科技)**.

The solution combined:

- XGI graphics chip **`3210`**
  - hardware acceleration for **2D, JPEG, Motion JPEG, MPEG-4, MP3**
  - hardware JPEG decoding and 2D transition effects
- Genesys Logic **`GL824`**
  - multi-format flash-card reader
  - USB 2.0 OTG

The article states that several customers had already adopted the design and products were expected on the market in Q3 2007.

Source:
- https://ctimes.com.tw/DispNews/tw/USB/%E7%B9%AA%E5%B1%95%E7%A7%91%E6%8A%80/%E5%89%B5%E6%83%9F%E7%A7%91%E6%8A%80/%E9%BB%83%E5%8B%83%E7%82%BA/07052918076H.shtml

Independent GL824 documentation is consistent with the storage side of that design. GL824 is a USB 2.0 OTG controller with an embedded 8051, flash-card interface, ATA/ATAPI, 16-bit host interface/FIFO, external SDRAM option and firmware-upgrade support. Contemporary reporting says it entered mass production by 2006.

Sources:
- https://www.alldatasheetcn.com/datasheet-pdf/pdf/207025/GENESYS/GL824.html
- https://www.eet-china.com/archives/1330.html

ITE's 2009 annual report and a 2010-01-18 Micro-electronics report establish the corporate/technology lineage: after ITE merged XGI and other companies at the end of 2008, ITE's multimedia group continued DPF SoC development using XGI graphics technology. The 2010 report quotes ITE second-business-unit GM Huang Bo-wei (黃勃為), formerly quoted as XGI GM in the 2007 DPF announcement, describing an ITE DPF SoC integrating CPU/GPU and 3D acceleration.

- https://www.mem.com.tw/3d-%E8%81%AF%E7%B6%B2%E4%B8%8A%E8%BA%AB%E3%80%80%E6%95%B8%E4%BD%8D%E7%9B%B8%E6%A1%86%E8%A6%8F%E6%A0%BC%E5%8D%87%E7%B4%9A/

### Relevance to DP-700SH / DP-850SH / DP-1020SH

The media capabilities of the 2007 XGI 3210 reference design are unusually close to the 2010 FUJIFILM upper models: DP-850SH / DP-1020SH officially support JPEG, Motion-JPEG, MPEG-4 SP and MP3 (plus WAV). The FUJIFILM family also exposes a broad flash-card set and USB connection.

This makes **XGI/ITE multimedia lineage + Genesys storage-controller/reference-design lineage** a concrete board-inspection target. If board photos become available, search chip markings for XGI/ITE and Genesys `GL82x` parts rather than looking only for a modern `IT9834/IT9836` marking.

### Important correction / uncertainty

Current ITE public documentation for the **`IT983x`** family is from the 2021-era smart-display product line and describes a modern architecture (including RISC-V in the published block diagram). The public 2021 application table lists `IT9834` with 64 Mb DDR1 / display up to 480×480 and `IT9836` with 128 Mb DDR2 / display up to 1024×600.

Sources:
- https://soc.ite.com.tw/upload/2021_09_222/ITE%2ASoC%2Aof%2AAutomotive%2ASmart%2ADisplay_EN.pdf
- https://soc.ite.com.tw/upload/2021_09_222/ITE%2ASoC%2Aof%2AAutomotive%2ASmart%2ADisplay_CH.pdf

I could not find a dated 2009/2010 primary source assigning the names **`IT9834` or `IT9836`** to ITE's then-current DPF SoC. Therefore the existing `IT9834/IT9836` hypothesis must not be treated as a confirmed 2009-era part-number identification. In particular, modern `IT9834` documentation says display up to 480×480, which does not match the 800×480 DP-700SH/DP-850SH panels.

### Assessment

**Confirmed:** XGI + Genesys had a shipping-customer DPF reference design in 2007; XGI's DPF/media technology and Huang Bo-wei moved into ITE's post-2008 multimedia organization; ITE was publicly discussing a DPF SoC in January 2010.

**Plausible but unconfirmed:** DP-700SH/850SH/1020SH may use a descendant of the XGI/ITE multimedia platform, and a Genesys `GL82x` controller may appear on the board.

**Do not assert:** that DP-700SH contains XGI `3210`, Genesys `GL824`, ITE `IT9834`, or ITE `IT9836` until a board marking, BOM, firmware identifier, or product-specific engineering record directly ties one of those parts to the FUJIFILM/Sharp hardware.
