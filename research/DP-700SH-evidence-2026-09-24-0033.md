# DP-700SH family evidence — 2026-09-24 00:33 JST

## Confirmed

### DP-1020SH launch was delayed because of a power-connection defect

AV Watch reported on 2010-03-10 that FUJIFILM postponed the DP-1020SH launch from 2010-03-13 to 2010-03-20 because **some units had a defect in the power connection section (「一部の機体に電源接続部の不具合」)** and all units therefore had to be inspected.

The same report explicitly says that the DP-850SH remained scheduled for 2010-03-13. It describes DP-1020SH and DP-850SH together as LED-backlit models with 2 GB internal memory and still-image/video/audio playback, while distinguishing their LCD sizes/resolutions.

Source:
- AV Watch, 2010-03-10, 「富士フイルム、フォトフレーム『DP-1020SH』を発売延期」
  https://av.watch.impress.co.jp/docs/news/353844.html

## What this supports

- The 2010 SH family was not completely identical at the physical-product level: a production/inspection issue was announced specifically for DP-1020SH while DP-850SH was not delayed.
- This is useful when comparing boards or power-input assemblies: even if DP-1020SH and DP-850SH share firmware/software architecture, their power-entry hardware or its production implementation may differ.

## Not established / inference only

- The report does **not** identify the failed component, PCB revision, connector manufacturer, AC adapter, ODM, or SoC.
- It does **not** prove that DP-1020SH uses a different main PCB from DP-850SH or DP-700SH. The defect could have been mechanical, assembly-related, connector-specific, or confined to a particular lot.
- It does **not** establish a Taiwan ODM.

## Reverse-engineering implication

If board photos become available, compare the DC input jack, input protection, nearby regulators, PCB revision/silkscreen and connector daughterboard arrangement between DP-1020SH and DP-850SH/DP-700SH. A DP-1020SH-specific revision around the power-entry section would be consistent with the contemporary recall/inspection evidence, but must not be assumed in advance.
