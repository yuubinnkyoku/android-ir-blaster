# DP-700SH evidence — 2026-09-08 23:08 JST

## DP-1020SH only: pre-release power-connection defect and all-unit inspection

### Confirmed

AV Watch reported on 2010-03-10 that FUJIFILM postponed the DP-1020SH launch from 2010-03-13 to 2010-03-20 because some units had a defect in the power connection section (`電源接続部の不具合`) and FUJIFILM would inspect all units (`全数検査`). The same report explicitly states that the DP-850SH would launch as scheduled on 2010-03-13.

Source:
- AV Watch, 2010-03-10, 「富士フイルム、フォトフレーム『DP-1020SH』を発売延期 ― 20日に。『一部電源部不具合による全数検査のため』」
  - https://av.watch.impress.co.jp/docs/news/353844.html

A separate launch article states that DP-1020SH and DP-850SH share most major functions, while their LCD size/resolution and chassis dimensions differ. It also confirms that all three 2010 models use an AC adapter.

Source:
- デジカメ Watch, 2010-02-02, 「富士フイルム、LEDバックライト採用のデジタルフォトフレーム」
  - https://dc.watch.impress.co.jp/docs/news/346441.html

### Interpretation / circumstantial evidence

This is useful negative evidence against treating DP-700SH / DP-850SH / DP-1020SH as completely identical hardware with only different LCD panels. The fact that DP-1020SH alone required an all-unit inspection for a power-connection defect while DP-850SH was unaffected implies at least one model-specific difference in the power-input / connector / mechanical implementation, or in the assembly process around that section.

This does **not** prove that the main PCB, SoC, OS, or firmware architecture differ. It is fully compatible with a shared software platform and partially shared electronics plus model-specific power/chassis parts.

### Connection to existing evidence

Existing evidence already shows:
- DP-700SH uses SHARP `EP-D72F` / ATECH `T04A-05200D2-S2`.
- DP-850SH uses SHARP `EP-D82F` / ATECH `T04A-05200D2-S3`.
- The three models received the same IrSimple/IrSS/IrDA hang fix, but through model-specific `TH34_dpf.pkg`, `TH35_dpf.pkg`, `TH36_dpf.pkg` files.

The DP-1020SH launch defect therefore strengthens a more nuanced model: a common 2010 DP software/product family with non-trivial per-model hardware variants, especially around display, chassis, and power-input implementation.

### Still unconfirmed

- The exact defective DP-1020SH component or connector.
- Whether the issue was on the main PCB, a daughterboard, DC jack, cable/harness, AC adapter, or mechanical assembly.
- DP-1020SH's standard AC-adapter model number.
- Any main-PCB revision caused by the pre-release inspection.
- SoC / OS / PCB identity across DP-700SH / DP-850SH / DP-1020SH.
