# DP-700SH investigation evidence — 2026-09-23 02:22 JST

## New evidence: predecessor DP-70SH exposes a remote-only firmware update entry sequence

### Confirmed

Digital Camera Watch's 2009-04-01 report on FUJIFILM DP-70SH firmware Ver.1.04.00 documents the firmware-update entry procedure in detail. With the version screen open under `本体情報の表示`, pressing the supplied remote buttons in this order switches to the firmware rewrite screen:

`拡大` → `縮小` → `時計` → `回転`

The update image is placed on a formatted SD card. This establishes that FUJIFILM's immediately preceding Sharp-ASV digital photo frame used an undocumented/maintenance-style remote key sequence to enter its firmware writer.

Source:
- Digital Camera Watch, 2009-04-01, 「富士フイルム、7型デジタルフォトフレームのファームウェアを公開」
  - https://dc.watch.impress.co.jp/cda/accessories/2009/04/01/10602.html
  - reports DP-70SH Ver.1.04.00 and the exact `拡大→縮小→時計→回転` sequence.

The same article confirms DP-70SH had a Sharp ASV 800×480 panel, IrSimple, and an SD-card firmware update mechanism. A later hands-on review describes DP-70SH as FUJIFILM's first digital photo frame.

Additional source:
- https://dc.watch.impress.co.jp/cda/item/2009/05/08/10786.html

### Relevance to DP-700SH

DP-700SH's remote has the same four labeled functions needed by this sequence (`拡大`, `縮小`, `時計`, `回転`) among its controls. Therefore the DP-70SH sequence is directly testable on a DP-700SH without needing a working firmware file.

This is especially useful because the user's DP-700SH is confirmed to expose `本体情報の表示` and currently reports Ver.1.03.00, while the public DP-700SH updater was Ver.1.04.00 (`TH34_dpf.pkg`).

### Hypothesis / NOT confirmed

- DP-700SH may have inherited the DP-70SH firmware-writer entry sequence.
- If the sequence opens a rewrite screen on DP-700SH, that would be strong evidence of continuity in the maintenance UI / software platform across the 2009→2010 Sharp-manufactured FUJIFILM photo-frame line.
- It would still NOT prove identical SoC, OS, bootloader, or package format.
- Do not attempt an actual rewrite without a verified DP-700SH image; merely entering/exiting the screen is the safe initial test.

### Why this is meaningful

This gives a concrete, non-destructive experiment on the existing DP-700SH hardware and may expose additional version/build information or updater behavior before `TH34_dpf.pkg` itself is recovered.
