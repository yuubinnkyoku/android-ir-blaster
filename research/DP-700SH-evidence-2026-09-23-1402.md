These are direct observations from the archived firmware bytes, not guesses from product specifications.

## Interpretation / hypotheses — NOT confirmed for DP-700SH

1. `SHARP_PF100_PLATFORM` is strong evidence that the DP-70SH firmware was built on an internally Sharp-named platform, apparently `PF100`.
2. Together with DP-70SH's Sharp manufacturing attribution and the later DP-700SH/850SH/1020SH `TH34/35/36` firmware naming, this makes a Sharp firmware/platform lineage substantially more plausible than a mere use of a Sharp LCD panel.
3. The binary's explicit `ATMEL` / `MXIC` serial-flash handling suggests the predecessor platform supported serial flash from Atmel and Macronix. It does **not** prove which flash chip was populated in DP-70SH, and says nothing yet about DP-700SH's populated flash.
4. The partition-like labels `BOOT`, `NVRAM`, `ROMTAB`, `STRTAB`, `FIRMWARE`, `VERSION`, `CHECKSUM` give concrete targets for reverse engineering the firmware container/layout.
5. No conclusion should yet be drawn that DP-700SH uses `PF100`, the same SoC, the same board, or the same RTOS. The next decisive test is to recover `TH34_dpf.pkg` or dump the DP-700SH flash and search for `SHARP_PF100_PLATFORM`, related platform names, and compatible ROM-table/layout signatures.

## Important negative result

Wayback CDX still returns no capture for the known DP-700SH/850SH/1020SH binary URLs:

- `http://download.fujifilm.co.jp/pub/tools/dp700sh/TH34_dpf.pkg`
- `http://download.fujifilm.co.jp/pub/tools/dp850sh/TH35_dpf.pkg`
- `http://download.fujifilm.co.jp/pub/tools/dp1020sh/TH36_dpf.pkg`

So this run recovered the predecessor binary, not the DP-700SH binary itself.
