## New useful hit

Taiwan trademark record `098027401` / registration `01391873` for 勝捷光電股份有限公司 (SanJet Technology Corp.) was filed on **2009-06-26** and explicitly includes **數位相框 (digital photo frames)** among Class-009 goods.

Source:
- https://www.findcompany.com.tw/trademark/01391873_098027401

The mirror states that its trademark data source is Taiwan's Ministry of Economic Affairs Intellectual Property Office.

SanJet company-registration cross-check:
- https://www.findcompany.com.tw/en/SANJET%20TECHNOLOGY%20CORP.
- Registration date: 2008-11-20
- Importer/exporter original registration date: 2009-06-03

Interpretation: the filing was made only 25 days after the 2009-06-01 AIPTEK/天瀚 manufacturing-business transfer effective date, so digital photo frames were already an explicitly claimed SanJet product category at the start of its post-transfer ODM phase. This supports, but does not prove, continuity of AIPTEK-era DPF/multimedia capability into SanJet.

## Classification

- **Confirmed:** SanJet filed a 2009-06-26 trademark whose goods explicitly included digital photo frames.
- **Circumstantial:** DPF capability appears to have been part of SanJet's intended commercial scope immediately after the AIPTEK business transfer.
- **Unproven:** the FUJIFILM DP-700SH / DP-850SH / DP-1020SH were designed or manufactured by SanJet; SoC/OS/PCB/IR protocol remains unidentified.
