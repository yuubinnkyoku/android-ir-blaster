## New evidence

AV Watch, 2010-03-10:
- DP-1020SH launch postponed from 2010-03-13 to 2010-03-20.
- Reason: some units had a defect in the power connection section (`電源接続部の不具合`).
- FUJIFILM performed an all-unit inspection (`全数検査`).
- DP-850SH was explicitly unaffected in schedule and launched on 2010-03-13 as planned.

Source:
- https://av.watch.impress.co.jp/docs/news/353844.html

Supporting launch specification source:
- https://dc.watch.impress.co.jp/docs/news/346441.html

## Assessment

Confirmed fact: DP-1020SH alone had a pre-release power-connection defect severe enough to trigger an all-unit inspection and a one-week delay; DP-850SH was not delayed.

Inference: the 2010 DP family should not be modeled as completely identical hardware with only different panels. At minimum, model-specific power-input / connector / chassis implementation or assembly differences existed or are strongly implied. This is compatible with the already observed model-specific AC-adapter variants on DP-700SH/DP-850SH and the model-specific TH34/35/36 firmware packages.

Do not infer from this alone that the main PCB, SoC, OS, or software architecture differ.
